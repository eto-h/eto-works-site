kotoVim

Vim-style editing in the text fields you already use.

AI can draft the text. The finishing touches are yours. Jump to the word you want and rewrite it with a few keystrokes. Try the editing tricks that make Vim look like magic, right in Word and Outlook.

Using the development build

Get started

  1. Click inside a sample document in Notepad and check that Normal appears.
  2. Press i to enter Insert and type a few words.
  3. Press Esc or Ctrl+[ to return to Normal. F8 also works.
  4. Use w / b to move between words and ciw to rewrite the word under the caret. Use v to select text and u to undo an edit.
  5. Choose Quit from the tray.
