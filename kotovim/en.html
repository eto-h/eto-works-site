Using the development build

Get started

  1. Open a sample document in Notepad and place the caret. Wait for Normal.
  2. Press i to type a few words. Left Alt keeps its normal Windows or app behavior.
  3. Use F8 to return to Normal, then w / b / e to move, diw to delete the whole word, and u to undo.
  4. Choose Quit from the tray.
