How to download for free on Work.ink

Work.ink opens after you accept the terms. The free download requires no payment or card details.

  1. Scroll past the paid plans and Google Pay to Complete 1 step to unlock your link. If you see a countdown, stay on that page until it ends.
  2. Choose one free task from the list and complete it. If an ad offers an extension or another program, choose Close or close the ad tab. You can get the app without installing anything else.
  3. On the Work.ink completion screen, choose Go To Destination. On the eto works page, check the app and file name, then choose Download EXE for free.

Ads may have large buttons labeled “Download” or “Windows extension.” Download your app from the final eto works page.
